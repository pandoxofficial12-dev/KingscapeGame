What is Kingscape?

Kingscape is a premium party card game built for unforgettable nights, legendary challenges and hilarious moments with friends.

How To Play

Shuffle the deck.
Draw a card.
Follow the rule shown.
Respect The Cup.

Official Rules

A — Waterfall

Everyone starts drinking. No player may stop until the player before them has stopped.

2 — You

Choose a player to drink.

3 — Me

You drink.

4 — Floor

Everyone touches the floor. Last player loses.

5 — Guys

All guys drink.

6 — Girls

All girls drink.

7 — Heaven

Everyone points to the sky. Last player loses.

8 — Follow Me

The player controls the finger. Everyone must copy. Last player loses.

9 — Rhyme Time

Whoever repeats, makes a mistake, takes too long or says nothing loses.

10 — Category

Whoever repeats, makes a mistake, takes too long or says nothing loses.

J — Never Have I Ever

Players who have done it lose.

Q — Question Loop

Only questions are allowed. Whoever fails loses.

K — Kingscape Cup

Add your drink to the cup. The last King drinks the entire cup.
