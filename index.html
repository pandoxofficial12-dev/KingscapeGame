Game Rules

A — Dominion Cards

Cards: Wave • Control • Flow • Chaos

Rule: Everyone drinks while you drink. When you stop, everyone stops.

2 — Call Out Cards

Cards: Call Out • Marked • Pick • Locked

Rule: Choose any player. They must take 2 shots.

3 — Bottoms Up Cards

Cards: Bottoms Up • Penalty • Rhythm • No Mercy

Rule: Take 3 shots.

4 — Group Cheers Cards

Cards: Group Cheers • King's Choice • Dance Floor • Take It

Rule: Take 2 shots and give 2 shots to any player.

5 — Ladies Cards

Cards: Ladies • Queens • Girls Night • She Rules

Rule: All women drink.

6 — Men Cards

Cards: Man Up • Men Drink • Bro Code • Man's Turn

Rule: All men drink.

7 — Last Hand Up Cards

Cards: Seven Heaven • Last One Up • Last To Raise • Last Hand Up

Rule: Last player to raise a hand takes 1 shot.

8 — Follow Me Cards

Cards: Follow Me • Control Point • Copycat • Trap

Rule: Last player to copy the finger action takes 1 shot.

9 — Rhyme Time Cards

Cards: Rhyme Time • Word Duel • Flow Game • Last Word

Rule: Players must say rhyming words. Mistakes take 1 shot.

10 — Category Cards

Cards: Quick Think • Category King • Brain Rush • No Repeats

Rule: Name items from a category. Mistakes take 1 shot.

J — House Rule Cards

Cards: House Rule • Last Call • The Law • The Decree

Rule: Create a new rule everyone must follow.

Q — Question Cards

Cards: Question Loop • Don't Answer • Keep Asking • Wrong Move

Rule: Ask questions without answering. Failure takes 1 shot.

K — King's Cup Cards

Cards: The Cup • Last King • Royal Mix • The Curse

Rule: Add your drink to the King's Cup. The last King drinks it all.
